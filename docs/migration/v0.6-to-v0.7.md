# Migration guide: v0.6 → v0.7

v0.7.0 is a breaking minor release of `dnd5e-engine`, `dnd5e-srd-data` and
`nat20-bridge`, in lockstep. It makes `GridScene` the only spatial backend,
drops the duplicate concentration `SaveRolled`, and closes the gaps a grid host
meets first. Each section below is one chunk of that work; every seeded pin a
chunk moved is listed with its reason.

## C24 — grid movement and reactions

### What a host has to do

1. **Expect opportunity attacks on the grid.** On v0.6 no move on a
   `GridScene` ever drew one. Now every walk does, whoever drives it: a
   character's or a host-driven foe's `move` intent, the monster AI's closing
   walk and its flee. Render `IntentSubmitted(intent_type="reaction")` and
   `AttackRolled(is_opportunity_attack=True)` mid-move.
2. **Handle a split `ActorMoved`.** A `move` intent still emits one
   `ActorMoved` for an uninterrupted walk, but a step that provokes closes the
   run walked so far: the attack lands between two `ActorMoved` events, so a
   host can place the hit. The monster AI's walks emit one per step, as before.
3. **Name the weapon a character strikes with** if your specs carry no
   `equipment`. A character's opportunity attack uses the new
   `PartyMemberSpec.opportunity_attack_weapon_id`, else the first melee weapon
   in `equipment`, else an Unarmed Strike (a flat 1 + Strength modifier,
   Bludgeoning). `start_combat` raises `ValueError` for an
   `opportunity_attack_weapon_id` that names no melee weapon.

### Behaviour changes

- **One trigger per step.** A step provokes a creature when it takes the mover
  from inside that creature's reach to outside it. Reach is Chebyshev, so
  stepping around inside it never provokes. The reactor must be the mover's
  enemy, conscious, not Incapacitated, with its Reaction, able to see the
  mover, and not Charmed by it (SRD 5.2 Charmed: "You can't attack the
  charmer"). Disengage now suppresses attacks from either side; it used to
  protect only a character from monsters.
- **Summons make none** (unchanged): a summon takes no reactions.
- **The walk stops** where an attack drops the mover to 0 HP or its Speed to 0.
  A character knocked unconscious no longer finishes its move. A mover a Push
  mastery hit carries off also stops there, keeping its unspent movement,
  rather than finishing the step the push interrupted.
- **Reach.** A character reaches with its weapon (10 ft with the Reach
  property), or with `reach_ft` when it makes an Unarmed Strike. A monster
  reaches with its first non-ranged stat-block attack: its own range if that
  attack is a melee attack with one (a Pike's 10 ft), else 5 ft. A
  melee-or-thrown attack's range is its throw, not its reach (SRD 5.2 Spear:
  "reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft.").
- **The attack goes through the activity context**, like one on the
  reactor's own turn: cover, Fighting Styles, Martial Arts, proficiency,
  enchantments, weapon mastery, Sneak Attack and the target's readied Shield
  all apply. A monster or a transformed creature uses its stat-block attack, so
  `AttackRolled.modifier` / `roll_total` and `DamageApplied.amount` /
  `damage_type` / `source_id` change: a weapon's slug, `"unarmed-strike"`, or
  `None` for a stat-block attack (the on-turn convention). A template-less foe,
  or a character when your asset loader carries neither its weapon nor
  `unarmed-strike`, still swings its `attack_bonus` / `damage_dice`
  (`source_id="synth:legacy-swing"`). Cleave,
  the Light property's extra attack, Loading and Bardic Inspiration don't apply.
  A Versatile weapon swings its one-handed die, so Great Weapon Fighting
  doesn't apply to it.
  A template monster's to-hit is still its spec's `attack_bonus` (+0 by default).
- **A template monster's opportunity-attack damage now reports
  `DamageApplied.source_id=None`**, the on-turn convention; it was
  `"synth:legacy-swing"`.
- **A foe whose `monster_template_slug` doesn't resolve makes no opportunity
  attack at all**, matching its on-turn behaviour; it used to swing its
  legacy `attack_bonus` / `damage_dice` fields regardless.
- **A hidden reactor that makes an opportunity attack stops being hidden**
  (SRD-correct; new).
- **Prone at range.** An opportunity attack on a Prone creature more than
  5 ft away has Disadvantage (it used to assume 5 ft).
- **Sneak Attack is once per turn of any creature.** The spent flag now clears
  at every turn start, so a rogue's opportunity attack on another creature's
  turn can deal it.
- **Monsters flee on the grid.** A monster under its flee threshold runs as far
  from its nearest enemy as its movement allows: reachable, unoccupied cells
  only, never through an enemy's space, ties broken by the cheaper route then
  the lower column and row. It takes no Disengage or Dash, so leaving a
  character's reach provokes. A cornered monster holds. An Incapacitated
  monster takes no flee stance (`has_fled` stays `False`).

### New API

- `PartyMemberSpec.opportunity_attack_weapon_id: str | None = None`.
- `GridTopology.reachable_cells(start, budget_ft, *, avoid=())` →
  `{cell: (cost_ft, previous_cell)}`: every cell a creature can walk to within
  a movement budget (difficult terrain costs double), for planning a route.

## C25 — zone-graph removal; one event per concentration save

### What a host has to do

1. **Pass a `GridScene`.** `start_combat` requires `grid_scene=`. Passing
   `scene_zones=` raises `TypeError`, and `grid_scene=None` raises
   `ValueError("start_combat: grid_scene is required")`, both before any combat
   state exists. Every `PartyMemberSpec.zone_id` / `EncounterMemberSpec.zone_id`
   must be one of the scene's in-bounds, unblocked cells (`cell_id(col, row)`).
2. **Port your zone layouts** with the recipe below.
3. **Replace the removed names** (table below).
4. **Read a concentration save from `ConcentrationCheck`.** The damage-triggered
   concentration save no longer emits a `SaveRolled(ability="con")` beside it.
   A dice display, a list of save targets or a save counter that read the
   `SaveRolled` now reads `ConcentrationCheck`, which carries the same `dc`,
   `roll_total`, `succeeded`, `natural`, `modifier`, `advantage` and `sources`.
5. **Delete a warning filter** for the `start_combat(scene_zones=`
   `DeprecationWarning` if your test configuration has one: nothing raises it.

### Removed names

| Removed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| `start_combat(scene_zones=...)` | `start_combat(grid_scene=GridScene(...))` |
| `SceneTopology`, `ZoneEdge` (from `dnd5e_engine`, `dnd5e_engine.specs` and `dnd5e_engine.orchestrator`) | `GridScene` (`dnd5e_engine.orchestrator.__all__` now exports it, so a star import still brings every type `start_combat` takes) |
| `dnd5e_engine.testing.ZoneGraph` | `dnd5e_engine.spatial.GridTopology(GridScene(...))`, which answers `within_range`, `distance_ft` and the rest of `SpatialTopology` |
| the `SaveRolled(ability="con")` a concentration save emitted beside its `ConcentrationCheck` | `ConcentrationCheck` |

`LiveCombatView`, every event and every spec keep their zone-named fields:
`zone_id`, `target_zone_id`, `actor_zone`, and `from_zone` / `to_zone` on
`ActorMoved` and `CombatantMoved`. On the grid each holds a cell id.

### Porting a zone layout

1. Give each zone a block of cells: its members stand on mutually adjacent
   cells, a 2×2 block for up to four creatures, so every pair is 5 ft apart. For
   a block at `(c, r)` use `cell_id(c, r)`, `cell_id(c, r + 1)`,
   `cell_id(c + 1, r)` and `cell_id(c + 1, r + 1)`, in that order.
2. Place the blocks of two zones joined by an edge of *d* feet along one row so
   that their nearest cells are ⌈*d* / 5⌉ cells apart: the closest creatures of
   the two zones then stand *d* feet apart (rounded up to the next 5 ft).
3. Make the `GridScene` wide and tall enough for every block.
4. Never seat two creatures on one cell. The 0.6 stopgap,
   `GridScene(width=len(zones), height=1)` with `zone_id = cell_id(i, 0)`, puts
   a zone's members on one cell; `start_combat` doesn't refuse that yet, but no
   move can ever produce it.

A former zone combat now gets everything the grid resolves: occupancy (a move
can't end on a creature, and enemies block the route), routes of many cells in
one `move`, reach-based opportunity attacks, areas of effect shaped by their
templates rather than by zones, cover, line of sight and light from the scene,
a summon seated on a free cell, pushes that move their target, and monsters
that flee.

### Behaviour changes

- **The zone graph's fallbacks are gone** with it: the single step to an
  adjacent zone, an area hitting every creature in a zone, a summon seated in
  its caster's zone, a push that moved nothing, and a fleeing monster that held.
  None of them ever applied on a grid.
- **An area the engine can't map onto the grid** still affects only the
  creatures standing on its anchor cell: the named target's cell, else the
  caster's. That covers the five `wall` templates (Blade Barrier, Tsunami, Wall
  of Fire, Wall of Thorns, Wind Wall) and Confusion, whose size is a formula.
  The behaviour is unchanged; its log line now reads `aoe_template_unsupported
  … falling back to anchor-cell targeting`.
- **One event per concentration save.** Each damage-triggered concentration
  check emits one event fewer, so every later event in `event_log`,
  `narration_events` and `drain_pending_events` sits one position earlier per
  check. The order is now `ConcentrationCheck`, then `LegendaryResistanceUsed`
  when a Legendary Resistance converts the failure, then the drop events on a
  failure. The dice are unchanged: the twin never drew one.
- **Rage.** An enemy's concentration check no longer counts as a saving throw
  the barbarian forced (SRD 5.2 Rage: "Force an enemy to make a saving throw").
  The attack or save that dealt the damage already extends Rage, so the change
  shows only in a turn where nothing else extends it.
- **Repeat saves.** A failed concentration check can no longer stand in for the
  failed save behind a condition's end-of-turn repeat save. When a target
  concentrating on a spell of its own failed its concentration check during
  the resolution that applied the condition, the repeat save used to roll
  Constitution against the concentration DC; it now uses the spell's own save.
- **nat20-bridge narration** renders the check as its own line, `Elara rolls a
  concentration save: 14 vs DC 10 — succeeds.`, where it used to narrate the
  twin as `Elara rolls a con save: …`.
- **`ZoneTransit` is never emitted.** It stays in the `CombatEvent` union so a
  host that still imports it keeps working; a later breaking release removes it.

### Moved seeded pins

None. Every zone-graph test whose rule holds on a grid was ported with the
recipe above and keeps its assertions and its dice; the tests that pinned
zone-only behaviour were deleted. The tests that read the concentration
`SaveRolled` now read `ConcentrationCheck`, with the same values.
