# Migration guide: v0.6 → v0.7

v0.7.0 is a breaking minor release of `dnd5e-engine`, `dnd5e-srd-data` and
`nat20-bridge`, in lockstep. It makes `GridScene` the only spatial backend,
drops the duplicate concentration `SaveRolled`, and closes the gaps a grid host
meets first. Each section below is one chunk of that work; every seeded pin a
chunk moved is listed with its reason.

## C24 — grid movement and reactions

### What a host has to do

1. **Expect opportunity attacks on the grid.** On v0.6 no move on a
   `GridScene` ever drew one. Now every walk does, whoever drives it: a
   character's or a host-driven foe's `move` intent, the monster AI's closing
   walk and its flee. Render `IntentSubmitted(intent_type="reaction")` and
   `AttackRolled(is_opportunity_attack=True)` mid-move.
2. **Handle a split `ActorMoved`.** A `move` intent still emits one
   `ActorMoved` for an uninterrupted walk, but a step that provokes closes the
   run walked so far: the attack lands between two `ActorMoved` events, so a
   host can place the hit. The monster AI's walks emit one per step, as before.
3. **Name the weapon a character strikes with** if your specs carry no
   `equipment`. A character's opportunity attack uses the new
   `PartyMemberSpec.opportunity_attack_weapon_id`, else the first melee weapon
   in `equipment`, else an Unarmed Strike (a flat 1 + Strength modifier,
   Bludgeoning). `start_combat` raises `ValueError` for an
   `opportunity_attack_weapon_id` that names no melee weapon.

### Behaviour changes

- **One trigger per step.** A step provokes a creature when it takes the mover
  from inside that creature's reach to outside it. Reach is Chebyshev, so
  stepping around inside it never provokes. The reactor must be the mover's
  enemy, conscious, not Incapacitated, with its Reaction, able to see the
  mover, and not Charmed by it (SRD 5.2 Charmed: "You can't attack the
  charmer"). Disengage now suppresses attacks from either side; it used to
  protect only a character from monsters.
- **Summons make none** (unchanged): a summon takes no reactions.
- **The walk stops** where an attack drops the mover to 0 HP or its Speed to 0.
  A character knocked unconscious no longer finishes its move. A mover a Push
  mastery hit carries off also stops there, keeping its unspent movement,
  rather than finishing the step the push interrupted.
- **Reach.** A character reaches with its weapon (10 ft with the Reach
  property), or with `reach_ft` when it makes an Unarmed Strike. A monster
  reaches with its first non-ranged stat-block attack: its own range if that
  attack is a melee attack with one (a Pike's 10 ft), else 5 ft. A
  melee-or-thrown attack's range is its throw, not its reach (SRD 5.2 Spear:
  "reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft.").
- **The attack goes through the activity context**, like one on the
  reactor's own turn: cover, Fighting Styles, Martial Arts, proficiency,
  enchantments, weapon mastery, Sneak Attack and the target's readied Shield
  all apply. A monster or a transformed creature uses its stat-block attack, so
  `AttackRolled.modifier` / `roll_total` and `DamageApplied.amount` /
  `damage_type` / `source_id` change: a weapon's slug, `"unarmed-strike"`, or
  `None` for a stat-block attack (the on-turn convention). A template-less foe,
  or a character when your asset loader carries neither its weapon nor
  `unarmed-strike`, still swings its `attack_bonus` / `damage_dice`
  (`source_id="synth:legacy-swing"`). Cleave,
  the Light property's extra attack, Loading and Bardic Inspiration don't apply.
  A Versatile weapon swings its one-handed die, so Great Weapon Fighting
  doesn't apply to it.
  A template monster's to-hit is still its spec's `attack_bonus` (+0 by default).
- **A template monster's opportunity-attack damage now reports
  `DamageApplied.source_id=None`**, the on-turn convention; it was
  `"synth:legacy-swing"`.
- **A foe whose `monster_template_slug` doesn't resolve makes no opportunity
  attack at all**, matching its on-turn behaviour; it used to swing its
  legacy `attack_bonus` / `damage_dice` fields regardless.
- **A hidden reactor that makes an opportunity attack stops being hidden**
  (SRD-correct; new).
- **Prone at range.** An opportunity attack on a Prone creature more than
  5 ft away has Disadvantage (it used to assume 5 ft).
- **Sneak Attack is once per turn of any creature.** The spent flag now clears
  at every turn start, so a rogue's opportunity attack on another creature's
  turn can deal it.
- **Monsters flee on the grid.** A monster under its flee threshold runs as far
  from its nearest enemy as its movement allows: reachable, unoccupied cells
  only, never through an enemy's space, ties broken by the cheaper route then
  the lower column and row. It takes no Disengage or Dash, so leaving a
  character's reach provokes. A cornered monster holds. An Incapacitated
  monster takes no flee stance (`has_fled` stays `False`).

### New API

- `PartyMemberSpec.opportunity_attack_weapon_id: str | None = None`.
- `GridTopology.reachable_cells(start, budget_ft, *, avoid=())` →
  `{cell: (cost_ft, previous_cell)}`: every cell a creature can walk to within
  a movement budget (difficult terrain costs double), for planning a route.

## C25 — zone-graph removal; one event per concentration save

### What a host has to do

1. **Pass a `GridScene`.** `start_combat` requires `grid_scene=`. Passing
   `scene_zones=` raises `TypeError`, and `grid_scene=None` raises
   `ValueError("start_combat: grid_scene is required")`, both before any combat
   state exists. Every `PartyMemberSpec.zone_id` / `EncounterMemberSpec.zone_id`
   must be one of the scene's in-bounds, unblocked cells (`cell_id(col, row)`).
2. **Port your zone layouts** with the recipe below.
3. **Replace the removed names** (table below).
4. **Read a concentration save from `ConcentrationCheck`.** The damage-triggered
   concentration save no longer emits a `SaveRolled(ability="con")` beside it.
   A dice display, a list of save targets or a save counter that read the
   `SaveRolled` now reads `ConcentrationCheck`, which carries the same `dc`,
   `roll_total`, `succeeded`, `natural`, `modifier`, `advantage` and `sources`.
5. **Delete a warning filter** for the `start_combat(scene_zones=`
   `DeprecationWarning` if your test configuration has one: nothing raises it.

### Removed names

| Removed | Use instead |
|---|---|
| `start_combat(scene_zones=...)` | `start_combat(grid_scene=GridScene(...))` |
| `SceneTopology`, `ZoneEdge` (from `dnd5e_engine`, `dnd5e_engine.specs` and `dnd5e_engine.orchestrator`) | `GridScene` (`dnd5e_engine.orchestrator.__all__` now exports it, so a star import still brings every type `start_combat` takes) |
| `dnd5e_engine.testing.ZoneGraph` | `dnd5e_engine.spatial.GridTopology(GridScene(...))`, which answers `within_range`, `distance_ft` and the rest of `SpatialTopology` |
| the `SaveRolled(ability="con")` a concentration save emitted beside its `ConcentrationCheck` | `ConcentrationCheck` |

`LiveCombatView`, every event and every spec keep their zone-named fields:
`zone_id`, `target_zone_id`, `actor_zone`, and `from_zone` / `to_zone` on
`ActorMoved` and `CombatantMoved`. On the grid each holds a cell id.

### Porting a zone layout

1. Give each zone a block of cells: its members stand on mutually adjacent
   cells, a 2×2 block for up to four creatures, so every pair is 5 ft apart. For
   a block at `(c, r)` use `cell_id(c, r)`, `cell_id(c, r + 1)`,
   `cell_id(c + 1, r)` and `cell_id(c + 1, r + 1)`, in that order.
2. Place the blocks of two zones joined by an edge of *d* feet along one row so
   that their nearest cells are ⌈*d* / 5⌉ cells apart: the closest creatures of
   the two zones then stand *d* feet apart (rounded up to the next 5 ft).
3. Make the `GridScene` wide and tall enough for every block.
4. Never seat two creatures on one cell. The 0.6 stopgap,
   `GridScene(width=len(zones), height=1)` with `zone_id = cell_id(i, 0)`, puts
   a zone's members on one cell; `start_combat` doesn't refuse that yet, but no
   move can ever produce it.

A former zone combat now gets everything the grid resolves: occupancy (a move
can't end on a creature, and enemies block the route), routes of many cells in
one `move`, reach-based opportunity attacks, areas of effect shaped by their
templates rather than by zones, cover, line of sight and light from the scene,
a summon seated on a free cell, pushes that move their target, and monsters
that flee.

### Behaviour changes

- **The zone graph's fallbacks are gone** with it: the single step to an
  adjacent zone, an area hitting every creature in a zone, a summon seated in
  its caster's zone, a push that moved nothing, and a fleeing monster that held.
  None of them ever applied on a grid.
- **A zone name where a cell belongs is refused, never a crash.**
  `start_combat` raises `ValueError` for a start position that is not one of
  the scene's cells; a `move` to a zone name emits
  `MoveFailed(reason="unreachable")`; a Spiritual Weapon placed or moved
  there is refused with `out_of_range` (`CastFailed` on the cast,
  `AttackFailed` on a later Bonus-Action move); and a Summon Dragon placed
  there gets `CastFailed(reason="target_invalid")`. Nothing is spent or
  moved.
- **An area the engine can't map onto the grid** — the five `wall` templates
  and Confusion, whose size is a formula — affects its named target only: see
  C26a below.
- **One event per concentration save.** Each damage-triggered concentration
  check emits one event fewer, so every later event in `event_log`,
  `narration_events` and `drain_pending_events` sits one position earlier per
  check. The order is now `ConcentrationCheck`, then `LegendaryResistanceUsed`
  when a Legendary Resistance converts the failure, then the drop events on a
  failure. The dice are unchanged: the twin never drew one.
- **Rage.** An enemy's concentration check no longer counts as a saving throw
  the barbarian forced (SRD 5.2 Rage: "Force an enemy to make a saving throw").
  The attack or save that dealt the damage already extends Rage, so the change
  shows only in a turn where nothing else extends it.
- **Repeat saves.** A failed concentration check can no longer stand in for the
  failed save behind a condition's end-of-turn repeat save. When a target
  concentrating on a spell of its own failed its concentration check during
  the resolution that applied the condition, the repeat save used to roll
  Constitution against the concentration DC; it now uses the spell's own save.
- **nat20-bridge narration** renders the check as its own line, `Elara rolls a
  concentration save: 14 vs DC 10 — succeeds.`, where it used to narrate the
  twin as `Elara rolls a con save: …`.
- **`ZoneTransit` is never emitted.** It stays in the `CombatEvent` union so a
  host that still imports it keeps working; a later breaking release removes it.

### Moved seeded pins

None. Every zone-graph test whose rule holds on a grid was ported with the
recipe above and keeps its assertions and its dice; the tests that pinned
zone-only behaviour were deleted. The tests that read the concentration
`SaveRolled` now read `ConcentrationCheck`, with the same values.

## C26a — areas of effect for every intent kind; the corrected monster areas

### What a host has to do

1. **Handle `AreaTargeted`.** Every area a cast, item use or feature use
   places now emits one, before anything in it rolls: the actor, the source
   slug, the SRD 5.2 shape (`sphere`, `cylinder`, `emanation`, `cube`,
   `cone`, `line`), its size, its origin cell and aim, and the creatures it
   affects and spares. A host that matches `CombatEvent` exhaustively needs a
   branch for it; one that skips unknown types needs nothing. An attack
   never gets one. A `use_item` whose item has several activities gets one
   only when `activity_id` names the area activity, or when the area
   activity is the one the item charges automatically; a feature with
   several activities already needs `activity_id`.
2. **Decide whom an area "of your choice" spares.** Sleep, Weird, Slow, Mass
   Cure Wounds, the Pipes of Haunting, the Mace of Terror, Intimidating
   Presence and every other area whose activity sets `affects.choice` now
   spares the actor and its allies when it harms, and the actor's enemies when
   it heals. Pass `excluded_target_ids=()` to catch everyone in the area (up
   to six for Slow and Mass Cure Wounds), as a v0.6 cast did, or list the
   creatures to spare.
3. **Send `excluded_target_ids` only with an area of your choice.** On any
   other attack, cast, item use or feature use — a Fireball, a sword swing — it
   is refused with `target_invalid` (`AttackFailed` for an attack, `CastFailed`
   otherwise) before anything is spent, and so is an id that is not in the
   combat.
4. **Aim feature and item areas.** A Breath Weapon, the Horn of Blasting or a
   Javelin of Lightning's bolt needs `direction` or a `target_id`, as a cast
   already did; with neither it is refused with
   `CastFailed(reason="target_invalid")` before its use is spent.

### Behaviour changes

- **Feature and item areas** resolve against every creature in their area;
  v0.6 resolved them against the named target alone. Cover is measured from
  their point of origin, as for a cast.
- **"Of your choice"** (above) and **"each enemy" / "each ally"**
  (`affects.type`) are honoured. No character-side area in the corpus is typed
  "each enemy" or "each ally" today.
- **"Up to N creatures."** Slow and Mass Cure Wounds affect up to six
  creatures: the ones `target_ids` names, else the first six the default keeps,
  in initiative order. Phantasmal Force affects the one creature `target_id`
  names; v0.6 hit whoever stood in a 10-foot cube in front of the caster. More
  names than the count, a repeated name or a name not in the combat is refused
  with `target_invalid`; the named creatures are not checked against the
  template.
- **An attack roll is never an area.** The Torch's and the Hammer of
  Thunderbolts' templated attacks stay single-target.
- **A template the engine can't map** (the five `wall` spells; Confusion, whose
  size is a formula) affects its named target only, and nobody when the intent
  names none; it used to fall back to the caster's own cell. The log line
  reads `aoe_template_unsupported … falling back to the named target`.
- **nat20-bridge** narrates the event: `Elara's sleep fills a 5-ft sphere at
  5,5: catches Goblin 1, Goblin 2; spares Elara.`
- A monster's own area action, a monster's cast and a legendary action still
  resolve against one target; C26b, below, aims them.

### New API

- `PlayerIntent.excluded_target_ids: tuple[str, ...] | None = None`. Ids must be
  distinct and non-empty (`ValidationError` otherwise).
- `dnd5e_engine.events.AreaTargeted` and `dnd5e_engine.events.AreaShape`, in
  the `CombatEvent` union and `ALL_COMBAT_EVENT_TYPES`.

### Data changes (dnd5e-srd-data)

- `affects.choice` on Sleep, Slow, Mass Cure Wounds and the Mace of Terror's
  Wave of Terror; `affects.count: "6"` on Slow and Mass Cure Wounds.
- Recharge 5–6 on the adult blue, adult copper, ancient copper, ancient gold,
  ancient green and young red dragons' breaths and the Centaur Trooper's
  Trampling Charge (they had none, so those dragons never breathed: a dragon now
  opens with its breath) and on the Tarrasque's Thunderous Bellow (it had 6).
- The adult gold, ancient gold and gold wyrmling Fire Breath and the magma
  mephit's are Cones; the adult copper's Acid Breath is a 60-foot Line, the
  adult gold's Cone 60 feet, the ancient gold's and the ancient green's 90 feet.
- The four green dragons' and the iron golem's Poison Breath affect each
  creature (`affects.type: "creature"`), not only enemies.
- A recharge with no formula recharges on a 6, Foundry's own default.

### Moved seeded pins

None. Every existing test keeps its dice. The one corrected monster a test
fights, the magma mephit, now breathes a Cone, but a monster's area still
resolves against one target; no test casts a spell whose choice flag or count
changed, or stands a second creature in a feature or item area; and the Pipes
of Haunting tests stand one foe in range, whom the choice default still picks.
The unmappable-template test was renamed (`…_targets_the_anchor_cell_only` →
`…_targets_the_named_target_only`) with its same single save.

## C26b — monsters aim their areas; a Multiattack's recharge; Shield and saves

### What a host has to do

1. **Expect `AreaTargeted` on monster turns.** A monster's breath or other area
   action, its stat-block spell and its legendary action now emit one before
   anything in it rolls. `source_id` is the action's slug (`"fire-breath"`) or,
   for a spell, the spell's (`"fireball"`). A host that handles the event
   (C26a) needs nothing more. `IntentSubmitted.target_id` on a monster's turn
   still names the creature the AI targets; read where the area lies and whom
   it affects from `AreaTargeted`.
2. **Re-check fixtures that put a monster beside its foes or far from them.** A
   monster never places an area that affects itself, nor one that affects no
   enemy: a Mage beside its only foe casts Cone of Cold rather than a Fireball
   centred on that foe, and a dragon whose breath reaches no one walks instead.

### Behaviour changes

- **A monster's areas resolve against the creatures in them** — its stat-block
  actions, its stat-block spells and its legendary actions; v0.6 resolved them
  against one target. The AI aims from the cell it stands in and takes the
  placement that affects the most enemies minus allies, never itself; ties go
  to fewer allies, then to the foes nearest the area's centre line, then to a
  fixed order (directions clockwise from north, Sphere centres in initiative
  order). Whom it affects follows the activity's `affects`, as for any actor:
  "each enemy" spares the monster's allies, and an area of your choice spares
  its side. Cover is measured from the area's point of origin.
- **Whom it affects includes a dying foe.** A monster's area affects every
  creature it catches there, a creature at 0 Hit Points included (SRD 5.2: an
  Unconscious creature auto-fails a Dexterity save, and damage at 0 HP is a
  death saving throw failure); only a choice or counted area spares the
  monster's charmer. The AI itself scores candidate placements only against
  creatures above 0 HP.
- **Areas have a reach.** A Sphere or Cylinder centres on a foe the monster can
  see within the action's or the spell's range; a Cone, Cube, Line or
  Emanation reaches its own size from the monster. v0.6 landed a monster's area
  on its target at any distance.
- **An area that would affect no enemy is not an option.** The monster takes
  its next action (a charged breath stays charged), its next spell, or its next
  legendary action; with none left, `advance_monster_turn(..., legendary=True)`
  raises `IntentRejectedError("no_legendary_action")` and spends no use.
- **"One creature" keeps one target.** An action whose template counts one
  creature (the Aboleth's Dominate Mind, the Chuul's Paralyzing Tentacles) and a
  template the grid can't place still resolve against the AI's target.
- **Multiattack.** An action a Multiattack uses "if available" sits out while it
  isn't — once spent, the Doppelganger's Unsettling Visage no longer forces a
  Wisdom save every turn — and a Recharge action it uses is spent when it
  fires. An area it uses (the Sphinx of Valor's Roar) resolves against that
  area. When a Multiattack's description offers alternatives, it repeats only
  an attack the description names: the Djinni makes Storm Blade or Storm Bolt
  attacks rather than three Create Whirlwinds, and the iron golem, medusa,
  stone giant, storm giant, tarrasque, horned devil and bugbear stalker no
  longer repeat an action their Multiattack doesn't name. A description is no
  longer cut short at an item whose id starts with a capital letter (the
  goblin boss's Shortbow); the pirate's "two Dagger attacks" now joins exactly.
- **A Multiattack's fallback never repeats a Recharge or X/Day action.** Such
  an action joins a Multiattack only through the precise join, where
  availability gates it: the Aboleth's fallback now repeats Tentacle, or
  Consume Memories at 30 ft, never its 2/Day Dominate Mind, and the Ancient
  Gold Dragon's Multiattack now makes Rend within reach or Weakening Breath at
  60 ft, not a second Fire Breath; nor does a fallback repeat an area that
  would affect no enemy from where the monster stands: beyond its breaths'
  90 feet the Ancient Gold Dragon walks in to Rend.
- **A readied Shield answers attack rolls only.** A monster's save action (a
  Breath Weapon) leaves it readied, its Reaction and slot unspent.
- **nat20-bridge** narrates a monster's area with the existing line:
  `Adult Red Dragon's fire-breath fills a 60-ft cone at 5,5: catches …`.

### New API

- `dnd5e_engine.areas.best_aim`, `AreaAim` and `AIM_DIRECTIONS`: the placement
  the monster AI uses, for a host that wants it for its own creatures.
- `dnd5e_engine.activities.monster_actions.expand_action_to_parts`: a monster
  action's activities, each with the stat-block action it comes from.

### Moved seeded pins

None. A monster area with one creature in it draws what its single target
drew, so every existing test keeps its dice, the legendary-action and
magma-mephit tests included. Seven tests now set their monster outside its own
area or their target within its reach — the stat-block-spellcaster scenario
and two Fireball unit tests (the Mage 30 ft from the hero), the adult red
dragon's breath, Fireball and Multiattack sequence, the polymorphed Giant Ape,
the Lich's legendary action that drops a summon, and the self-centred breath
test — with every assertion and every draw unchanged. The polymorphed-monster
test's ids gained a `column` parameter.

## C27 — monster senses; the condition clauses

### What a host has to do

1. **Expect templated monsters to see.** A foe with a `monster_template_slug`
   now has its stat block's Blindsight, Darkvision, Tremorsense and Truesight.
   In a scene with `lighting` or `obscurement_cells`, or against an Invisible
   creature, its attacks, its opportunity attacks, its own Dodge and Hide
   follow the SRD: a Goblin Warrior attacks a character in Darkness without
   Disadvantage, a Bat's Blindsight sees an Invisible character, and a
   character can no longer Hide in Darkness from a Goblin Warrior within 60
   feet — `submit_player_intent` raises `IntentRejectedError("target_invalid")`
   before any roll. Pass `EncounterMemberSpec(senses={})` to keep
   a creature without senses, or any `senses` to give an un-templated creature
   its own.
2. **Drop host-side condition immunities if you like.** A templated foe whose
   spec leaves `condition_immunities` empty takes its stat block's: a Skeleton
   can't be Poisoned, a Ghost can't be Grappled. A non-empty list still wins
   outright.
3. **Expect more `MoveFailed(reason="frightened")`.** A Frightened creature
   can't move closer to its fear source even when it can't see it.

### Behaviour changes

- **Frightened's Disadvantage on ability checks applies only while its source
  is in sight**, as its attack-roll half already did: a `CheckActivity` rolled
  by a creature Frightened of a source it can't see rolls one d20, not two.
- **Poisoned and Frightened reach Hide and escaping a grapple.** Hide's Stealth
  check and an `escape_grapple` check roll at Disadvantage (`CheckRolled`
  sources `["condition:attacker"]`, a second d20) for a Poisoned creature, or
  one Frightened of a creature it can see.
- **Frightened's no-approach rule has no sight clause** (SRD 5.2: "You can't
  willingly move closer to the source of fear"). An unknown, dead or untracked
  source still imposes nothing.
- **Petrified resists Poison damage** (SRD 5.2: "Resistance to all damage")
  instead of being immune to it, and has Immunity to the Poisoned condition:
  a Poisoned rider never lands on a Petrified creature, and one it already had
  imposes nothing while it is Petrified.
  `project_passive_damage_modifiers(["petrified"])["immunities"]` is now `[]`.
- **One immunity check on every action and effect path.** A creature immune
  to a condition (`condition_immunities`, or Petrified's grant) never gets it
  from an effect's rider, a seeded effect, Grapple, Shove's Prone or Hide's Invisible.
  Grapple's and Shove's save still rolls; a Shove's push still lands.
- **`LiveCombatView.initiative[*].conditions` now lists one entry per
  effect imposing a condition at runtime too** (seeded stacking already
  did). For display, dedupe by name or read `active_conditions`, which
  stays deduplicated.
- **A condition two effects impose outlives the first one's ending by a
  successful repeat save or a concentration drop** (a timed expiry already
  kept it), on both stores: when two casters' Hold Person hold one creature
  and the first caster's concentration ends, it stays Paralyzed in
  `active_conditions` too.

### New API

- `EncounterMemberSpec.senses: CombatantSenses | None = None` — `None` takes
  the template's senses; a value (a dict coerces) wins.
- `dnd5e_engine.rules.conditions.CONDITION_GRANTED_IMMUNITIES` and
  `granted_condition_immunities(conditions)`: the immunities a condition
  grants (Petrified → Poisoned).
- A keyword-only `fear_source_in_sight: bool = True` on
  `conditions_grant_disadvantage_on_ability_checks` and
  `project_passive_check_modifiers`, matching
  `conditions_grant_advantage_on_attack`'s.

### Moved seeded pins

None. Every existing test keeps its dice: no fixture fights a templated monster
in Darkness or against an Invisible creature, or rolls Hide or an escape while
Poisoned or Frightened. Two assertions changed with the rule they pinned, each
test renamed with its dice unchanged: the Frightened mover facing a source in
Darkness is now stopped (`test_frightened_mover_cannot_approach_source_it_cannot_see`),
and Petrified projects Resistance without Poison immunity
(`test_petrified_projects_resistance_to_all_damage_and_no_immunity`).
